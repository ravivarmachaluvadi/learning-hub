Lab 5 · btree-and-lsm

Recorded results — 2026-08-28, Apple Silicon Mac, JDK 25

Ratios matter, not absolute values. Toy scale (4-key pages, 6-key memtables) — real engines change the constants, never the shape.

Exp1Amplification.java — 3,000 puts (1,000 distinct keys), 500 gets

Scorecard B-tree LSM
WRITE amp (disk bytes / user bytes) 119.2 (a whole 8 KB page per ~100 B row) 2.5 (WAL + flush + compaction)
READ amp (places checked per get) 6.0 (= height, flat) 2.0 considered, 0.9 real probes (blooms skip the rest)
SPACE amp 1.40 (page slack; fill 71%) 1.39 before compaction → collapses after

LSM: 13 flushes, 3 compactions, 3 tables → 1 after final compact; read amp after compaction 0.9 probes/get. Neither engine wins both columns.

LSM same writes: 22,000 vs 24,000 bytes — order-blind. Bloat bonus: deleted 2,500 of 6,000 keys → pages 3,746 → 3,746, zero reclaimed (what VACUUM exists for).

Exp3Bloom.java — 8 SSTables, 2,000 gets for absent keys

disk probes per get
blooms OFF 16,000 8.0 (every table, every time)
blooms ON 270 0.14

False-positive rate 1.69% (10 bits/key ≈ ~1–2% by design); 98.3% of disk I/O eliminated by a few KB of RAM per table.

ExpDebugBroken.java — the planted bug (exercises rung 6)

Engine answers before compaction after
Lsm (correct) k01=v31, k00=null (deleted) identical — invariant 4 holds
BrokenLsm k01=v31, k00=null k01=v1, k00=v0 — 10 answers changed

One changed expression makes compaction pick the oldest version: deleted keys resurrect, recent updates silently revert. No crash, no error, no log line — which is why the invariant is written down and tested. Pointing LsmTest's fresh() at BrokenLsm gives 5 passed, 1 failed: only compaction_preserves_every_answer_and_reclaims_space notices.
